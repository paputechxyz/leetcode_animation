680. Valid Palindrome II — Two Pointers (with one delete)

Squeeze two pointers inward while the ends match. At the first mismatch you get one delete: try dropping the left char, or dropping the right char, and check whether what's left in between is a palindrome.

Why only check the inside? Everything outside the mismatch has already matched in pairs, so it stays a palindrome whatever happens in the middle. Only string[left..right] still needs checking.

      
Why only two options? At the first mismatch, s[left] ≠ s[right], so one of these two characters must go. Deleting anything else leaves this bad pair in place. So we try deleting s[left] (check left+1..right) and deleting s[right] (check left..right-1).

      
or short-circuits: if option 1 already returns True, option 2 is never run.

      
Index ranges, not slices. The helper takes left, right instead of copying string[a:b], so no new strings are made.

    

    

      
TimeO(n)
        n = len(string). The main scan plus at most two helper scans, each over the not-yet-checked middle (fewer than n chars), so at most about 3 × n/2 comparisons.

      
SpaceO(1)
        Only integer indices. The helper gets left/right numbers rather than sliced copies, and the call depth is at most 2.
